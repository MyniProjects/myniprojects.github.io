Privacy Policy

Publisher and privacy contact

This policy applies to the Google Play version of Globe Quiz: Flags & Capitals for Android. Myni Projects, based in Poland, publishes the game and is the controller for the personal data it handles under this policy. For privacy requests, questions and support, email myniprojects@gmail.com.

Gameplay data and backups

The game does not require a game account. Answers, game history, progress and settings are stored on your device. Depending on your Android settings, Android may back up selected app data to your Google account or transfer it to another device. If you export a backup, you choose where to save it and whom to share it with. If you enable reminders, the game schedules them on your device. You can turn reminders off in the game or in Android settings.

Optional usage analytics

If you turn on Usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app usage events, such as screens visited, game modes and difficulty levels, quiz duration, summary results and outcomes of rewarded ad attempts. It may also process technical information about the device and app and an app installation identifier. We use these data to understand how the game is used and improve it. The game disables advertising ID collection and advertising personalisation signals in Firebase Analytics.

Optional crash reporting

If you turn on Crash reports, Firebase Crashlytics receives technical details to help us diagnose and fix problems. Reports may include stack traces, app and Android version, device details and installation identifiers. If both options are enabled, reports may also include recent analytics events. When you first agree to send reports, the game may also send reports already stored on your device. The game can save a report locally before you make your choice.

Analytics and sending crash reports are off by default. You can turn either option on or off separately in Settings → Analytics and crash reports. These choices do not affect gameplay. Turning off an option withdraws consent for future processing based on that consent. It does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing or automatically delete data already sent.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob for native, full-screen and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process IP address and approximate location inferred from it, device and app information, advertising and other device or app identifiers such as the app set ID, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. They use these data to deliver and measure ads, personalise ads where permitted, diagnose problems and prevent fraud. Which ads and processing apply depends on your location, device and privacy choices.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to present and record advertising privacy choices where applicable. To review or change an available choice, open Settings → Advertising privacy options when that item is shown. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. These controls are separate from the game’s analytics and crash reporting choices. Even when ads are non-personalised, limited data may be processed for delivery, measurement or fraud prevention as permitted by law. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Android limits other apps’ access to the game’s private storage. The Firebase and Google Mobile Ads SDKs encrypt data in transit. You can reset gameplay progress in the game or remove local app data through Android settings or by uninstalling. Manage Android backups and exported files separately. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting to remove them from live and backup systems. User-level and event-level Analytics data are retained according to the project’s retention setting. Advertising and consent data retention depends on the purposes, settings and retention rules of the providers involved. We keep support correspondence for as long as necessary to resolve the matter, comply with legal duties or handle related claims. See Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google Analytics data retention.
