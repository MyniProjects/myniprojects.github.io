Privacy Policy

Data controller and contact

The data controller is MyniProjects.

For privacy inquiries, contact us at myniprojects@gmail.com

Information we process

DartCall is an app for tracking darts scores and statistics. Player profiles, names you enter, scores, game history, and settings are stored on your device. They may be included in an Android backup or transferred to another device, depending on your system and Google account settings.

If you use voice input, the app asks for microphone permission and sends your speech to the system's speech recognition service. The app prefers on-device recognition, but depending on the available service, audio may be sent to its provider, such as Google. Processing depends on the selected service. You can revoke microphone permission in Android settings; scores can also be entered manually. The app does not send recordings or recognized speech to Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics.

We use the following Google services:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — with your consent, collects information about app usage, such as screens opened, game modes, number of players, and game duration, as well as installation identifiers and device information. We use this information to understand and improve the app. We do not send player names or profile and match identifiers to Analytics. You can change your consent in the privacy section of the app settings. Withdrawing consent stops further collection but does not automatically delete data already transmitted.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — automatically collects crash and error reports, diagnostic data, device and app version information, and installation identifiers. We use these to identify and fix problems. Reports may contain technical error messages and stack traces.
  • Google AdMob — displays ads and measures their performance. Google and its advertising partners may process information including your IP address, advertising ID, device information, and interactions with ads. Ads may be personalized depending on your choices and applicable law. Where required, the app displays a consent form; available privacy choices can be changed in the app settings. Advertising ID settings are also available in Android settings.
  • Google Play Billing — handles the purchase and restoration of ad removal. Google processes transaction data under its own privacy policy. The app receives information needed to verify and restore the purchase; it does not receive full payment card details.

Google and its partners may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, subject to safeguards required by law. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We process diagnostic data based on our legitimate interest in maintaining app stability and security. Data retention and deletion

Game data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. Backups may remain in your Google account according to your settings and Google's policies; you can manage them in your backup settings. Analytics, diagnostic, advertising, and purchase data is retained according to the relevant service's configuration, policies, and applicable law. To ask about deleting data processed by the controller, contact us at the email address above. Our ability to locate data may depend on the identifiers available.

We use safeguards to protect data, including Android's app data protections and encrypted connections used by Google services.
