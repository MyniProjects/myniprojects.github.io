Vehicle information and local data

FuelManager lets you record information about your vehicles and refueling, including vehicle details, fuel purchases, prices and mileage. The app uses this information to display your history, statistics and charts.

This information and your app settings are stored on your device and are not sent to the developer. They remain on your device until you delete them in the app, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.

Depending on your device and backup settings, Android may include app data in a backup and restore it when you reinstall the app or move to another device. You can manage or delete backups through your device or Google account settings.

If you choose to share information from the app, it is sent to the app or service you select.

Advertising

FuelManager uses Google AdMob to display ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising privacy choices.

Google’s advertising SDK collects and shares information such as IP addresses, which can indicate approximate location; device and account identifiers; app and ad interactions; and diagnostic information. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Collection and use can vary depending on your privacy choices, device settings and the advertising mode used.

According to Google, data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Where available, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices in the app’s Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. The app’s vehicle and refueling features remain available without advertising consent.

Google’s data handling, retention and deletion practices are described in its Privacy Policy.
