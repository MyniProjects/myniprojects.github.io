Privacy Policy

Who we are

AI Race is an Android game published by MyniProjects, based in Poland. MyniProjects is the controller for personal data handled by MyniProjects as described in this policy. Contact myniprojects@gmail.com for privacy questions, requests or support.

Game data on your device

AI Race saves your progress, in-game rewards, language and settings on your device. The game does not require you to create an account. Depending on your Android settings, the primary save and preferences can be backed up or transferred by Android. If you export or share a save, you choose where the copy goes. Anyone you send it to may be able to read or import it.

The game can schedule reminders on your device if you enable them. You can turn them off in the game or in Android settings.

Optional advertising and privacy choices

AI Race uses Google AdMob to offer optional ads that earn in-game rewards. The game requests non-personalized ads. Google and advertising service providers may process information such as your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses information for ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google’s User Messaging Platform manages privacy choices. When a privacy message applies, use the options shown in the message. To review or change a choice later, open Settings in AI Race and select Privacy options when that entry is available. You can also manage the advertising identifier in Android settings. Declining optional ads or changing privacy choices does not prevent ordinary play. Changes affect future processing and do not undo earlier lawful processing.

Optional crash reporting

If you choose to share crash reports, AI Race uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and freezes. Reports can include stack traces, app version, operating system and device details, and an installation identifier. We use these reports to investigate and fix stability problems. Google processes them on our behalf.

Reporting is off until you opt in. To withdraw consent for future reporting, turn off Crash reports in AI Race Settings. You can continue playing with reporting off. Firebase keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting the process of removing them from live and backup systems. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Google Play Games achievements

When you use a Google Play Games profile, AI Race sends selected achievement unlocks to Google so they can appear on that profile. Google Play Games processes profile information such as your gamer tag and avatar, along with SDK usage and diagnostic data, to provide and improve the service. AI Race stores your game save on your device. You can play without a Play Games profile and manage sign-in and Play Games data in your Google account settings. Google describes its privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We use data needed for the game features you choose to provide those features. If you contact us, we use the information you send, such as your email address and message, to respond and handle your request. Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are providing the service when the processing is necessary to do so, consent for advertising or storage and access technologies where consent is required, and legitimate interests in operating and securing the game and handling support where permitted. We also process information when necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

Google and its service providers may process advertising and privacy-choice data. Android backup and device transfer are governed by the services you use and your system settings. Information may be processed outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are subject to an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. You can ask us for information about applicable safeguards using the contact above.

Retention and deletion

Local progress and settings remain on the device until you reset the save, clear the app data or uninstall the game. Resetting the save affects game progress. Clearing app data also removes local settings. Manage or delete Android backups and any exported copies separately. Google retains data it processes under its published policies and service settings. We keep support messages for as long as needed to resolve the request, meet legal obligations or handle related claims.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data, restriction of processing or portability, and you may object to certain processing. You can withdraw consent at any time and exercise available regional privacy choices in the app. Contact us using the address above. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority or with Poland’s Personal Data Protection Office.

AI Race is intended for players aged 13 and over. We update this policy here when the game or its data practices change.

Progress, ads and rewards

Ads are optional. The game shows what you can earn before you choose to watch. You receive the reward when the ad provider reports that you earned it. A skipped or failed ad does not use up a reward claim. Ads depend on network access and availability. Each reward can have its own daily limit, cooldown or other in-game eligibility rule. Virtual currency, boosts and other rewards are for use in AI Race. They cannot be cashed out or transferred to other players.
