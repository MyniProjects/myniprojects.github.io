Privacy Policy

Publisher and contact

Myni Projects publishes Lickety Climb for Android and is responsible for the personal data it processes as described in this policy. For privacy requests, support or complaints, email myniprojects@gmail.com.

Game progress and Android backups

The game saves your progress, records and settings on your device without a game account. Depending on your device and system settings, Android may back up eligible game data or transfer it to another device. You can remove the local save by clearing the game’s storage in Android settings. Manage any copies in Android backups separately.

Daily and weekly events

To keep event schedules and resets consistent, the game requests the current UTC time from UTCTime over HTTPS. If that request fails, the game can ask Google Public NTP over unencrypted UDP to refresh the time for a previously confirmed event period. These requests send no game progress or persistent game identifier. The services receive your IP address as part of the connection. UTCTime keeps short-lived access logs for debugging, abuse prevention and rate limiting. Google Public NTP temporarily logs the full IP address for troubleshooting, service improvement and abuse detection and says it deletes those logs within 30 days. See the privacy notices for UTCTime and Google Public NTP.

Optional analytics and crash reports

Usage analytics and crash reporting are off by default. You can enable or disable either feature in Settings → Privacy and keep playing with both off.

If you enable Share usage analytics, Google Analytics for Firebase receives app activity, such as app opens, screens and sessions, app and device information, and an app instance identifier. Google derives approximate location from the IP address. Myni Projects uses these statistics to understand how the game is used and to improve it. Analytics collection of the Android advertising ID and Analytics consent for advertising are disabled. Google retains event and user-level data according to the Analytics property settings.

If you enable Send crash reports, Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash details, such as stack traces, app and Android versions, device details and installation identifiers. Myni Projects uses these reports to diagnose crashes and freezes. A report may be sent the next time you open the game while reporting is still enabled. When analytics is also enabled, a report may include app activity leading up to the error. Google keeps crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting to remove them. Firebase uses encrypted connections for these uploads. See Google’s Firebase privacy notice.

Ads and your privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob to offer rewarded ads. You can choose whether to watch them. To check consent, prepare and deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, Google and, where applicable, its advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic information and identifiers such as App Set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts on the device. The game disables personalised ad requests and removes permission to access the Android advertising ID. Google’s ads SDK encrypts its data in transit.

Where the Google consent form is available, the game shows it when applicable. If Ad privacy options appears in Settings → Privacy, use it to review or update your choices. Google keeps ad-related data under its own privacy and retention practices. See Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s retention practices and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.
